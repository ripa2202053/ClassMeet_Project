Who Is It For?

ClassMeet is tailored to meet the specific requirements of every participant in the educational ecosystem — students, teachers, and institutional leaders.

For Students

Enjoy a distraction-free, privacy-protected virtual auditorium. Join in seconds with just a room code or link, participate on collaborative boards, and receive transparent attendance credit without having personal data logged.

For Teachers & Professors

Focus 100% on teaching instead of micromanaging attendance spreadsheets or dealing with software installation headaches. Seamlessly annotate slides, sketch complex diagrams, and see genuine student comprehension cues.

For Universities & Colleges

Scale effortlessly from intimate seminars to 500-student auditorium lectures. Ensure academic integrity for remote exams without invasive software, and integrate directly with Canvas, Blackboard, and Moodle via LTI 1.3.

For K-12 & Training Academies

Safeguard student data with strict COPPA and FERPA adherence. No student video feeds are stored or monetized, and intuitive moderation controls prevent unauthorized disruptions or screen hijacking.

Find the right solution for your classroom

Whether you are an individual educator or a large university department, ClassMeet scales to your needs.
