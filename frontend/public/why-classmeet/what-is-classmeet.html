What is ClassMeet?

ClassMeet is an AI-powered smart classroom ecosystem purpose-built for synchronous education, peer collaboration, and verified engagement — replacing fragmented corporate video tools with a unified learning theater.

Academic-First Virtual Space

Generic video conferencing was engineered for sales calls and standups. ClassMeet replaces corporate tiles with an interactive lecture amphitheater where teachers teach, students interact naturally, and classroom etiquette is preserved.

Seamless Zero-Install Access

No mandatory executables, background daemons, or cumbersome software installs. Students and instructors join through modern browser standards via WebRTC, WebGL, and WebSockets on any device with zero friction.

Privacy-Preserving On-Device AI

All intelligent analytics — from eye blink authentication to focus tracking — execute entirely inside the student's local browser sandbox. No facial biometrics or raw video streams are ever stored on cloud servers.

Live Collaborative Immersion

Synchronized vector whiteboards, lockstep slide decks with laser pointer trails, and instantaneous doubt-clearing cues empower teachers to facilitate real dialog rather than one-way broadcasts.
