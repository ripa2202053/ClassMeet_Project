OUR IDENTITY

What Makes Us Different?

We built an intelligent virtual classroom engine, not a glorified webcam grid. Here is how our architecture stands completely apart from legacy web conferencing.

On-Device Edge Machine Learning

Unlike platforms that transmit video to central servers for facial analysis, our MediaPipe neural network executes locally on the student's GPU via WebGL, guaranteeing zero biometric latency and absolute privacy.

Sub-50ms Peer Mesh Streaming

Our WebRTC selective forwarding unit (SFU) dynamically negotiates bitrates and routes packets across regional nodes, eliminating robotic audio glitches and video freezing even on constrained mobile hotspots.

Contextual Pedagogical Tools

We integrate continuous slide synchronisation, instant mathematical formula rendering, live collaborative whiteboards, and doubt raising right into the main stream rather than buried in side chat tabs.

One-Click LMS Synchronization

Verified presence rosters and session engagement metrics stream automatically into your institution's LMS gradebook without manual CSV exports or post-lecture administrative busywork.
