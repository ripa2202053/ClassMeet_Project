THE INSPIRATION

Why We Built It

Addressing the chronic friction points of remote learning: awkward roll calls, proxy presence, invasive surveillance software, and clunky separate tools that pull students away from real education.

Ending the "Roll Call" Dilemma

Instructors routinely waste 10 to 15 minutes of every lecture taking manual attendance or reviewing spreadsheet logs that are easily faked with static photos. ClassMeet automates this completely without touching cloud storage.

Eliminating Spyware Anxiety

Traditional proctoring software demands kernel-level access, scans personal disks, and records intimate bedroom environments. We proved that ethical browser-based edge intelligence can verify academic integrity without invasive breach of privacy.

Unifying Disconnected EdTech

Teachers previously had to juggle video conferencing apps, separate whiteboard tabs, external slide viewers, and attendance forms. ClassMeet converges every pedagogical component into one ultra-fluid, latency-free interface.

Empowering Educator Agency

Technology should serve pedagogy, not hinder it. We designed ClassMeet hand-in-hand with university professors and K-12 instructors to ensure the platform feels like an empowering academic studio.
